## PCA / Harmony / PC robustness
Use identical gene-only singlets, log normalization (10,000 scale), 2,000 variable-feature selection, and no regression of condition. Organelle genes remain in counts/DE but are omitted from the embedding HVGs. Run 50 PCs and Harmony by sample (package 2.0.5, theta=2, lambda=1, up to 20 iterations; converged after 5). With samples nested in condition, stronger mixing is not by itself evidence of a better biological representation.

At resolution 0.5, examine 10/15/20/25/30/40/50 dimensions in each branch. Unintegrated ARI(20,25)=0.809, ARI(25,30)=0.880; Harmony corresponding ARIs=0.886 and 0.903. Select 25 dimensions as a parsimonious point in this local stability region for branch comparison, not because the old result also used 25. The 40–50 PC partitions are a second locally stable region and differ materially from 25 PCs; report this limitation and retain all assignments. Unintegrated 25 PCs happens to give 18 clusters; Harmony gives 17. Neither number is asserted or used to choose annotation.

Compare nearest-neighbor replicate mixing within condition and confident atlas-signature groups (normalizing for unequal replicate size), canonical expression, cluster atlas scores, condition neighborhoods, and stress-marker neighbor smoothing. All are descriptive diagnostics. Cell-level metrics do not create biological replication. The underlying RNA expression and counts are identical across branches, but grouping can change DE.

## Pseudobulk and contextualization (planned before viewing new DE)
Sum raw gene counts by population × biological sample. Primary population inclusion: at least 50 singlets in each of the four samples; show the >=25 and >=100 eligibility sensitivity. Two biological replicates per condition means exploratory inference. No pairing is established; use DESeq2 ~condition, stress/control. Restrict to the original gene-only common feature universe and retain row-total >=10. Report fitted, valid-p-value, and finite-adjusted-p-value counts separately. Significant: padj<.05 and |log2FC|>=1; no requirement to reproduce old DE counts.

For enrichment, primary universe is genes with nonmissing DESeq2 padj within each population (eligible for the significance decision). Fisher table: P1 vs non-P1 crossed with stress DEG vs not stress DEG; one-sided enrichment, BH across populations within each branch/test family. Also report the all-prefilter-result universe as sensitivity, where independently filtered/NA padj genes cannot be called significant. Check P1 drought-only control genes from local P1 WT results and remove interaction candidates, with a stricter nonsignificant-interaction version if available. Non-significance is not proof of no interaction. Neither control addresses all expression/power confounding.

Seven P1 GO rows are existing annotations, not new P3 GO enrichment. Distinct genes, complete zero combinations, and pairwise overlap must be reported. P1 BRL3×drought and P3 WT root-tip sorbitol stress remain contextualization, not validation of interaction or effect direction.

## Decisions after branch evidence, before DE
Keep unintegrated as the primary analysis and Harmony as sensitivity. Mean within-condition/atlas-group replicate mixing ratio improves from 0.876 to 0.984, but broad atlas neighbor consistency is essentially unchanged (0.485 vs 0.485); same-condition neighbors fall from 0.859 to 0.600 and sparse stress-marker smoothing correlation falls from 0.229 to 0.184. This indicates loss of condition-associated geometry, not proof of biological overcorrection. There is no ground truth here that disentangles all condition and technical effects. Raw RNA counts/expression remain unchanged; final DE uses them for both branches. Retain both full result sets and characterize membership-dependent sensitivity.

Use broad lineage labels only where atlas + canonical evidence supports a shared identity across developmental/condition states. Keep cluster-level confidence in the annotation table; merging does not make weaker subclusters certain. For example, Pericycle merges the control-enriched and stress-enriched unintegrated states based on XPP/PPP signatures and canonical markers. Do not perform their DE as different cell types. Root_cap is intentionally broader than LRC or columella. Meristematic_unresolved and Epidermal_initial_like remain low confidence and cannot support precise cell-type claims. Strong cell-cycle evidence supports Dividing_meristematic as a state, not a lineage. Phloem and small unresolved/sample-enriched groups remain descriptive only when sample eligibility fails.

The 25-PC annotation mapping is bound to the saved partition file's MD5. A future changed partition must trigger new evidence review. No fixed number of clusters or populations is an analysis invariant.
